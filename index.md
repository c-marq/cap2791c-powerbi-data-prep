## Welcome

Raw data is rarely ready for a report. Files arrive with missing values, text that looks the same but is not, numbers stored as words, and tables that do not line up. This book teaches you to turn files and databases into a model you can trust, using Power BI Desktop on Windows.

You will work with one story from start to finish. Sabor Miami is a small group of food trucks around Miami. Its owner has questions about sales, tips, menu items and staff, and the records behind those questions come from CSV files, Excel workbooks, text files and a database. Each chapter takes one step toward answering the owner with numbers that hold up.

## Who this book is for

This book is for students and working people who are new to Power BI. If you have used Excel (PivotTables, tables and filters) and seen a little SQL (SELECT, WHERE, JOIN and keys), you already have the habits this book builds on. No coding background is needed.

## How the book works

The book follows the Zero Principle of Data Analytics: know your data before you analyze it, and analyze it in a fixed order.

1. State the business objective.
2. Load and view the data.
3. Understand each feature (each column).
4. Analyze one column at a time (univariate).
5. Pre-process the data: clean, transform and combine it.
6. Analyze two columns at a time (bivariate).
7. Analyze three or more columns together (multivariate).

Each chapter teaches one part of that order, and each section ends with a short Try This activity so you can practice right away.
